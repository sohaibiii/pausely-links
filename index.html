Questions people ask first

Will Pausely lock me out of my apps?

No. An ordinary pause is a moment, not a wall. After it you choose: I changed my mind, or continue into the app for a few minutes. The only things with no way through are ones you set up yourself, such as a daily limit you have used, a block you started, or a rule that blocks an app during certain hours. Each of those ends on its own.

How is this different from Screen Time limits?

A Screen Time limit waits until your time is used up and then offers Ignore Limit. Pausely steps in every time you open the app, before the scrolling starts, and asks for a breath first. The longer comparison.

Which apps does it work with?

Any app on your iPhone or iPad. You pick them in Apple's own Screen Time picker, so it works with whichever ones take your time. Pausely never learns their names. Apple hands it private codes instead.

Does Pausely see what I do in other apps?

No. It never sees your browsing, your messages or anything inside another app. iOS tells it how long the apps you chose have been open, and Pausely keeps those minutes on your device.

Is Pausely free?

Yes, and free is a working product, not a demo: two apps protected, one rule, the breathing pause, your whole progress screen, today's time in each app you chose, and the countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign up, no email field and no password anywhere in the app.

Which devices does it need?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or iPadOS 17, or newer. There is no Mac version, because the Screen Time frameworks Pausely is built on are not available there.
