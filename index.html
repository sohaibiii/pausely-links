Questions people ask first

Will Pausely lock me out of my apps?

No. An ordinary pause is a moment, not a wall. After it you choose: I changed my mind, or continue into the app for a few minutes. The only things with no way through are ones you set up yourself, such as a daily limit you have used, a block you started, or a rule that blocks an app during certain hours. Each of those ends on its own.

How is this different from Screen Time limits?

A Screen Time limit waits until your time is used up and then offers Ignore Limit. Pausely steps in every time you open the app, before the scrolling starts, and asks for a breath first. The longer comparison.

Which apps does it work with?

Any app on your phone, so it works with whichever ones take your time. On iPhone and iPad you pick them in Apple's own Screen Time picker, and Pausely never learns their names: Apple hands it private codes instead. On Android you pick them from the list of apps on your phone.

Does Pausely see what I do in other apps?

On iPhone and iPad, no. It never sees your browsing, your messages or anything inside another app. iOS tells it how long the apps you chose have been open, and Pausely keeps those minutes on your device. On Android, Pausely uses the Accessibility service to see which app is in front. It never reads your messages or what you type, and never taps or types for you. In Chrome and a few other browsers it also sees the address of the site you open, so it can pause the sites you chose. It asks first, and explains all of this before you say yes.

Is Pausely free?

Yes, and free is a working product, not a demo: two apps protected, one rule, the breathing pause and your whole progress screen. On iPhone and iPad it also covers today's time in each app you chose, and the countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign up, no email field and no password anywhere in the app.

Which devices does it need?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or newer, or an Android phone running Android 8 or newer. There is no Mac version.
