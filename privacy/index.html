The short version

Pausely collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs and no server. It makes no network calls of its own. Everything it knows is written to your iPhone and stays there.

Pausely does not even know which apps you chose to pause. Apple's Screen Time gives it opaque tokens that only your phone can interpret. There is no app name, no identifier, and nothing to send.

This policy applies to the Pausely app for iPhone, published by TOPSOL, and to this website. It is written to be read, and it says the same thing as the privacy label on Pausely's App Store page: Data Not Collected.

What we collect

Nothing. In Apple's terms, "collecting" means transmitting data off the device and storing it somewhere that is not transient. Pausely has no channel through which that could happen.

What stays on your phone

Pausely stores the following in its own private storage on your iPhone, protected by your device's passcode and encryption. None of it is uploaded, backed up by us, or visible to us.

  • Your app selection — as opaque Screen Time tokens Pausely cannot read (see below).
  • Your rules and schedules — the names you give them, their hours and their pause.
  • Pause outcomes — that a pause was shown, when, and whether you walked away, went in, or chose an alternative. This is what the progress screen, the streak and the chart are made of.
  • What you write — journal entries, the words you type on the pause screen, mood check-ins after a session, and the names of custom alternatives.
  • Settings — your chosen pause, strict-mode state, alternatives, and preferences.
  • Your Premium entitlement — a tier and an expiry date, so the app knows what to unlock.

If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted local backup, Apple may include Pausely's data in that backup under your own Apple ID and Apple's privacy terms. We have no access to it.

Screen Time and your app selection

Pausely is built on Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity). You grant it access once, through Apple's own prompt, and can revoke it at any time in Settings → Screen Time.

When you pick apps, Apple's picker returns tokens: values that identify the app to iOS but mean nothing to Pausely and nothing outside your phone. Pausely stores those tokens so it can ask iOS to shield the apps you chose. It never receives a bundle identifier or an app name, and it could not report one if it wanted to. Where it needs to compare tokens between its own components it compares one-way hashes of them, never the tokens themselves.

Pausely does not observe your browsing, your messages, or what you do inside any app. iOS itself draws the block screen and enforces the shield. Pausely learns only that its own pause was shown and what you chose on it.

Purchases

Premium is sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. The transaction is between you and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. Apple tells Pausely whether a purchase is active — nothing else — and Pausely tells Apple nothing about how you use the app. No usage data, no journal, no app selection and no identifier of ours travels with a purchase.

Notifications, widgets and the Lock Screen

Notifications are optional and are generated on your phone. Pausely uses them to hand you from the block screen into the pause, and to ask afterwards how a session left you feeling. No notification is sent from a server, because there is no server.

Widgets and the Live Activity show counts and dates — today's wins, your streak, time reclaimed, and how long an access window has left. They deliberately do not have Screen Time access and never see which apps you blocked. A Lock Screen countdown names the rule you wrote ("Bedtime"), never the app, because a Lock Screen is readable by anyone holding the phone.

Third parties

None. Pausely contains no third-party code, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporter. The only other party involved is Apple: it operates the App Store, processes purchases, enforces the Screen Time shield, and — if you have opted in to sharing with developers — may share crash logs with us under its own policy. We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone, because we hold none.

Deleting your data

Everything Pausely holds is erasable from inside the app, without asking us:

Settings → Your data → Reset all data
Empties every pause, win, rule, journal entry and check-in, takes the shields down, withdraws the schedules and starts you at the beginning. It refuses while strict mode is running — the one commitment you asked the app to hold you to — and it does not touch your subscription, which belongs to your Apple ID.

Deleting the app removes all of its data from the phone as well. There is nothing on our side to delete, and so nothing to request — but you are welcome to write to us anyway. See the Your data page.

Your rights

Wherever you live — including under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA — you have rights to access, correct, export, restrict and erase personal data a company holds about you, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Because Pausely holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to produce or delete; the data on your phone is already in your hands, and the app's own controls exercise every one of those rights for you. If you believe otherwise, write to topsol.org@gmail.com and we will respond.

Children

Pausely is a self-directed tool for the person using the phone. It is not a parental-control product and does not manage another person's device. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — of any age.

Changes we already know about

We are designing one optional feature that would change the sentence at the top of this page: an AI reflection that turns the "why are you opening it?" question into a short conversation. It is not in the app today. When it ships:

  • It will be off until you switch it on, and switching it off returns the app to making no network calls at all.
  • It will send only the display name of the app you are opening, three numbers (how many times today, the time on the clock, your streak) and the words you type into that one screen — to Pausely's own service, never a model vendor's API from the app.
  • It will never send your journal, your check-ins, your app selection or any identifier, and nothing sent will be linked to you.

This policy will be updated on the day that feature ships, before it is switched on for anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top moves when we do, and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes. Continued use of Pausely after a change means you accept the revised policy.
