How it works

A breath, then a decision.

Pausely doesn't delete your apps or lock them away. It steps in at the moment you open one, asks for ten seconds, and lets you decide with a clear head.

  1. Setup, once

    Choose the apps that take your time

    Pick them in Apple's own picker. Two are free, and Premium covers every app, website and whole category you choose.

    On iPhone and iPad, Pausely uses Apple's Screen Time, the same system behind iOS's built in app limits. It asks for that permission once, and you can take it back in Settings at any time.

    On Android it uses the Accessibility service to notice when one of your apps comes to the front. It explains what that lets it see before you say yes.

  2. When you tap one

    The block screen comes first

    Instead of the app, you see a number: how many times you have opened it today. No comment, no lecture. Only the number.

    Tap Open Pausely to take the pause, or Never mind to leave the app closed. iOS draws this screen, so the picture here comes from our App Store preview.

  3. Ten seconds

    A pause before you go in

    The breathing pause is the one everybody starts with: in for four seconds, hold for two, out for four.

    With Premium you can swap it for typing a short string, picking why you are opening the app, or reading a reminder. Or let Pausely pick a different one each time.

  4. Then

    You choose, on purpose

    I changed my mind closes it there, and Pausely counts the win.

    Under Continue for, tap 1m or 5m and the app opens for that long. Or take the third way out: one of the things you said you would rather do, such as a walk, a book or a glass of water, with a timer when you start it.

  5. If you go in

    The time is yours, and it ends

    A countdown runs while the app is open: on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island on iPhone, in a quiet notification on Android.

    When the time is up, the pause is back in front of the app. Nothing is taken from you. You are asked again.

    4:32
    Then the pause comes back.
  6. Over time

    Watch it add up

    Progress shows pauses today, the times you walked away, the time saved, your streak and a chart of the week. All of it is free.

    Time per app shows today's minutes in the apps you chose. With Premium, Pausely also shows your patterns over four weeks.

Gentle by default. Firm when you ask.

Some days a breath is enough. Some weeks you want a boundary that holds. You choose how strict Pausely is.

Setting it up takes a few minutes.

  1. Choose your apps. On iPhone, Apple asks for Screen Time access on the same screen. On Android, Pausely asks for Accessibility access. It can't work without it.
  2. Try your pause once, so you know what is coming.
  3. Add things you would rather do. A walk, a book, a glass of water, or something of your own.
  4. Allow notifications if you want them. Pausely says why it uses them first.
  5. Done. Open one of your apps and meet the pause.
