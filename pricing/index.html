The small print, in plain words.

What happens if my subscription ends?

What protects you keeps protecting you. Every app you chose and every rule keeps running and stays editable, and the pause you chose keeps standing in front of your apps. Adding more closes, and so do the extras that show you things, such as widgets and time banners.

How do the family plans work?

On iPhone and iPad, Family Yearly and Family Lifetime are shared through Apple's Family Sharing. Each person gets Premium in their own copy of Pausely, on their own device. Nobody sees anyone else's apps, numbers or journal.

Can I restore Premium on a new phone?

Yes. Open the Premium screen in Pausely and tap Restore purchases. Premium belongs to your Apple Account, or on Android to your Google account, not to the device.

Does Premium move between iPhone and Android?

No. Pausely has no accounts of its own, so Premium stays with the store you bought it in: the App Store or Google Play.

Are there ads in Pausely?

No. Pausely shows no ads, and Premium is the only thing it sells.
