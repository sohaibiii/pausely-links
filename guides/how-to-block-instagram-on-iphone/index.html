How to block Instagram on iPhone without deleting your account

Most people don't want to leave Instagram. They want their messages and their friends' photos, without the hour of Reels that comes attached. Here is how to block or limit it on an iPhone and keep your account exactly as it is.

By the Pausely team · Updated 27 November 2026 · 6 min read

None of these steps deletes or deactivates your account. Your profile, followers and messages stay where they are.

Option 1: Instagram's own daily limit

Open your profile, tap the menu at the top right, and look for Time management. On older versions it is under Your activity, then Time spent. There you can set a Daily limit, and a sleep mode that quiets notifications at night.

Where it stops working. When you reach the limit, Instagram shows a reminder you can dismiss in one tap. It tells you the time. It doesn't stop you.

Option 2: A Screen Time limit

  1. Open Settings and tap Screen Time. If it has never been set up, turn on App & Website Activity first.
  2. Tap App Limits, then Add Limit.
  3. Open the Social category and tick Instagram.
  4. At the bottom of the same list, open Websites, tap Add Website and type instagram.com, so the limit covers Safari too.
  5. Tap Next, set the time and tap Add.

Where it stops working. The cover that appears when your time is used has Ignore Limit For Today on it. A Screen Time passcode chosen by someone else, with Block at End of Limit switched on, closes that door.

Option 3: Delete the app and use the website

A popular middle path. Press and hold the icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Your account is not affected. When you need to check something, sign in at instagram.com in Safari.

The website does messages and the feed, and it is a little slower and clumsier than the app. That clumsiness is the feature: people tend to check and leave. Where it stops working: Reels play on the website too, and the app is twenty seconds away in the App Store.

Option 4: Block instagram.com in Safari

If you have deleted the app and the website has become the new habit:

  1. Open Settings, tap Screen Time, then Content & Privacy Restrictions, and turn the switch on.
  2. Tap Content Restrictions. On newer versions of iOS this row is called App Store, Media, Web & Games.
  3. Tap Web Content and choose Limit Adult Websites.
  4. Under Never Allow, tap Add Website and enter instagram.com.

This also turns on Apple's adult content filter and removes Private Browsing from Safari, which surprises some people.

Option 5: Keep the app, and put a pause in front of it

The options above are all or nothing, and Instagram is rarely all bad. You open it to answer a message and surface forty minutes later. The message was fine. The opening without thinking was the problem.

Pausely works on that moment. Tap Instagram and you see how many times you have opened it today, then a ten second breath. After it you choose: I changed my mind, or continue for a few minutes to answer that message. When the time is up, the pause is back, which is usually when you notice you had drifted into Reels.

With Premium you can also see about how many short videos your time came to. It is an estimate, and it tends to land.

A note on messages. If people reach you on Instagram, tell the ones who matter where else to find you before you block anything. It removes the best excuse for opening it.
